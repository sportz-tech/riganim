# RigAnim Studio Suite - 3D Character, Rigging & World Creation Suite

A professional, studio-grade Blender (4.x & 5.x) suite designed for rapid 3D character rigging, AI/Webcam motion capture, animation sequencing, multi-actor filmmaking, and procedural world building.

---

## 🌟 Included Suites & Features

### 1. 🦴 RigAnim Studio: Core Rigging & Animation Suite (`HumanRigGenerator`)
A procedural, 1-click human, creature, and world-building studio toolkit:
- **Interactive Alignment Markers**: 1-click interactive bone placement with symmetry mirroring.
- **Voxel Auto-Skinning**: 1-click automatic vertex weight calculation and seamless mesh deformation binding.
- **Blender 5.2 Slotted Action Native**: Forward-compatible layered action slots, preset library (Walk, Run, Jump, Idle, Combat), timing speed scaling, and F-curve jitter smoothing.
- **Continuous Multi-Action Management**: 1-click action trash deletion with auto-advancing selection flow.
- **Cross-Project Importers**: 1-click `.blend` file importing for saved actions, characters, rigs, and environment models.
- **Multi-Actor Manager**: 1-click character cloning with independent actions, materials, and conflict-free constraint retargeting.
- **Surface Spawn Points & Keyframe Travel**: Mark spawn locations directly on 3D terrain and animate character travel across timeline frames.
- **🌲 Asset & Mesh Spawner**: Interactive click-to-place world building for trees, houses, stones/rocks, and props with natural scale and rotation scattering.
- **🗡️ Prop & Tool Attacher**: 1-click snap & attach props (weapons, tools, phones, stones) with automated finger grasping and keyframed pick/drop.
- **🎥 Dialogue Cameras & Cinematic Lighting**: Automated framing (Wide, Medium, Close-up, OTS), dynamic orbit/zoom, multi-camera switching, and mood lighting presets.

### 2. 📹 RigAnim Mocap: AI Performance Capture (`MotionCaptureTransfer`)
Real-time webcam and video AI motion capture transfer for Blender armatures:
- **Live AI Tracking**: MediaPipe pose, face mesh, pupil gaze, and 5-finger tracking streamed directly into Blender over low-latency 60 FPS UDP sockets.
- **Facial Speech & Expression Transfer**: Real-time jaw speech sync, eye blinking, smiling, and eyebrow emotion tracking.
- **Keyframe Recording**: Live recording and baking of mocap performances directly onto the Blender timeline action editor.

## 🚀 Unified 1-Click Installation

1. Download **`RigAnimStudio_Suite.zip`** (or `HumanRigGenerator.zip`).
2. In Blender, go to **Edit** ➔ **Preferences** ➔ **Add-ons** (or **Get Extensions** in Blender 5.x) ➔ **Install from Disk...**
3. Select the `.zip` file and enable the checkbox.
4. Press `N` in the 3D Viewport to open the **`RigAnim Studio`** tab.
