================================================================================
          THE CANAL RESCUE: 10-SECOND 3D ANIMATION PRODUCTION MASTER PLAN
                  Based on True Viral Human Chain Rescue Story
================================================================================

PROJECT SPECIFICATIONS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Total Duration: Exactly 10.0 Seconds
* Project Frame Rate: 30 FPS (Frames Per Second)
* Total Frame Count: 300 Frames Total (Frames 1 to 300)
* Character Rigs:
  - Rescuer 1 (Hero in Canal) - Human Biped
  - Duplicated Rescuers (2 or 3 Characters on Wall for Human Chain) - Shift+D
  - Trapped Dog - Animal Quadruped Rig
* Setting: Steep concrete/sloped canal bank, slippery wet water line, canal ledge.

================================================================================
3-ACT CINEMATIC STORYBOARD & TIMELINE BREAKDOWN (30 FPS = 300 FRAMES)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ACT 1: THE TRAP & THE LEAP (0.0s to 3.0s | Frames 1 to 90)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theme: Urgency & Emotion

* Frames 1 to 35 (Dog's Panic & Hero Sprint):
  - Shot 1 (Wide/Medium Low): The dog is paddling frantically in the canal water,
    struggling to grip the steep, slippery concrete slope.
  - The hero notices the situation from the upper ledge and sprints to the edge.

* Frames 36 to 90 (The Vault & Jump In):
  - Shot 2 (Wide Cinematic): The man reaches the ledge, drops down, plants one 
    hand on the edge/railing, and performs an explosive vault leap down into the canal!
  - Frame 75-80: He splashes into the canal water and wades toward the dog.

* Rigging & Addon Execution:
  1. Hero Rig: Use "Run Loop" (Frames 1-35), then "Prop Jump & Cross / Rail Vault" (Frames 36-80).
  2. Dog Rig: Animate front paws paddling against water level.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ACT 2: THE PHYSICAL STRUGGLE & SLIPPERY WALL (3.0s to 6.0s | Frames 91 to 180)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theme: Weight, Slippery Surface, Frustration & Realization

* Frames 91 to 135 (Scooping the Dog):
  - Shot 3 (Water-Level Medium Shot): The hero reaches the dog, wraps both arms 
    underneath the dog's chest, lifting its head securely above water.
  - The dog calms down and clings onto the rescuer's shoulder.

* Frames 136 to 180 (The Failed Climb & Slip):
  - Shot 4 (Medium Low Angle looking up the slope):
  - The hero tries to climb up the steep concrete canal slope with one foot planted.
  - Due to the slippery algae/wet surface and the dog's heavy weight, his feet slip 
    backward down the slope into the water!
  - He looks up towards the top ledge, panting and realizing he cannot get out alone.

* Body Mechanics Notes:
  - Weight & Tension: Keep the hero's spine bent forward (carrying dog).
  - Slipping Foot: Keyframe CTRL-foot_IK slipping back quickly by 0.4m on the slope 
    with hips dropping suddenly to show lost traction.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ACT 3: THE HUMAN CHAIN & TRIUMPHANT RESCUE (6.0s to 10.0s | Frames 181 to 300)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Theme: Teamwork, Coordinated Pull, Emotional Payoff

* Frames 181 to 225 (Forming the Human Chain):
  - Shot 5 (High-Angle Down the Slope): 
  - 2 additional rescuers (duplicated character rigs) arrive at the canal ledge!
  - Top Rescuer anchors feet on solid ground and holds the wrists of Middle Rescuer.
  - Middle Rescuer leans down the slope, extending his arm towards the canal water.

* Frames 226 to 275 (The Hand Grip & Coordinated Haul):
  - Shot 6 (Close-Up / Medium Hero Cut):
  - The hero reaches up with one hand; the bottom rescuer's hand GRIPS his wrist!
  - All rescuers tense their backs and pull in unison (co-ordinated backward lean).
  - The hero and dog are lifted up the concrete slope, clear of the water!

* Frames 276 to 300 (Safety & Emotional Relief):
  - Shot 7 (Top Ledge 3/4 Medium Shot):
  - The man and dog are pulled over the ledge onto safe, dry ground.
  - The dog is set down, shaking off water droplets, while the rescuers smile 
    and catch their breath in triumph!

================================================================================
STEP-BY-STEP RIGGING & SETUP IN BLENDER:
================================================================================

1. HOW TO DUPLICATE THE CHARACTER FOR THE HUMAN CHAIN:
   - Select your character armature AND the character mesh together in Object Mode.
   - Press 'Shift + D' and move along X axis to create Rescuer 2 and Rescuer 3.
   - Each duplicated rig gets its own independent pose controllers!

2. HOW TO RIG THE WRIST-TO-WRIST HUMAN CHAIN:
   - For Rescuer 2 (holding Rescuer 1):
     Add a "Child Of" constraint on Rescuer 1's CTRL-hand_IK.R targeting 
     Rescuer 2's hand bone (or use standard Inverse Kinematics).
   - When the top rescuer pulls backward, the whole chain moves together smoothly!

3. ATTACHING THE DOG TO THE RESCUER:
   - On the Dog rig's root bone: add a "Child Of" constraint targeting the 
     hero's chest bone ('spine.003') with Influence = 1.0 starting at Frame 115.

4. 4 CAMERA CUTS ON TIMELINE:
   - Camera 1 (Sprint & Discovery): Frame 1
   - Camera 2 (Vault & Splash In):  Frame 36
   - Camera 3 (Slippery Wall Slip): Frame 136
   - Camera 4 (Human Chain Pull):   Frame 181
   - In HumanRigGenerator, bind each camera to its cut frame using Timeline Cuts!
